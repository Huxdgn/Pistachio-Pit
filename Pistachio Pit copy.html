PISTACHIO PIT

The room is packed with pistachios and they are all yours. They cost $0.

One catch: every shell stays in the room. Find the glowing green nuts by moving around the room and clicking them. You can spend 3 nuts to invite a friend who searches too.

Your goal is to get as many nuts as possible before time runs out. But you can leave the room whenever you want and switch to another snack. Leaving gives you points for the time you save. The question is not whether nuts still exist. It is whether the next nut is still worth the search.

ROOM CLOSED

The economics: the physical resource never vanished, but the cost of locating the next usable unit rose. Friends increased your search capacity, yet their success also produced more shells. That is why a resource can remain abundant while becoming uneconomic to extract.
